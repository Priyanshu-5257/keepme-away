KeepMe Away uses your device's front camera to measure the distance between your eyes and warn you if you're sitting too close to the screen. On Android, it shows a countdown and can block the screen until you move back to a healthy distance.

All processing happens entirely on your device — no images are saved, no data is uploaded, and no internet connection is required. Detection uses a bundled open-source model (MediaPipe BlazeFace) that runs locally.

Features:
• On-device proximity from eye keypoints (privacy-first)
• One-time calibration at your comfortable distance
• Full-screen warning countdown, then protective overlay
• Adjustable sensitivity and warning time
• Usage statistics (warnings, blocks, protected time)
• Lightweight and battery-friendly
• Works entirely offline

Permissions:
• Camera: measure the distance between your eyes on-device
• Display over other apps: show the warning and block overlay
• Notifications: keep protection running in the background
• Ignore battery optimizations (optional): improve reliability in background

Why use it?
• Reduce eye strain and fatigue
• Encourage healthy screen habits for kids
• Help students maintain good posture while studying
• For heavy phone users who sit too close without noticing
